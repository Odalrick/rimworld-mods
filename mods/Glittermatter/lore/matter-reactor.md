# The Matter Reactor

A seed of glittermatter, powered and directed. It is the only source of glittermatter there is.

## How it feeds

The reactor holds millions of distinct molecular machines. Given power and direction, they grow a network of leaching
tunnels down through the rock below, dissolve whatever useful elements they find, and carry them back to be built into
more of themselves. "Ambient matter" is the polite phrase; in practice it is eating the ground.

What comes out is stacked as blocks of gripping micromachines, the format the material is stored in everywhere else.

## Slow on purpose

It is neither fast nor efficient. It simply never stops, and it never asks anyone to do the work. A colony scales its
supply by building more reactors and feeding them more power, which is why a glittermatter operation looks like a power
farm with a few small boxes attached.

## Adaptation

Glittermatter is not one machine but thousands of designs, each suited to elements that happen to be abundant somewhere.
The design library is the valuable part, and a techprint is how it travels.

Adapting that library to the rock under a particular set of feet is the research. The control transmitters are simple
once you hold the programs — what the techprint buys is the adapted design library, not the ability to build a radio.

## Why the first one cannot be built

A reactor is made of glittermatter, and reactors are the only source of it. The first one is therefore bought or looted,
never manufactured. Holding one does not, by itself, let you make another: the machines reproduce themselves, but they
do not carry the library that would let them be re-adapted.

Exotic traders carry them. That is the entire supply chain.
